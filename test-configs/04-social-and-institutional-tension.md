# 04-social-and-institutional-tension

## Topic

Should elite universities use AI tutors aggressively, even if it changes what education feels like?
## Context

~~~text
Imagine a top university considering mandatory AI tutors for every course. The AI tutors can answer questions, generate practice problems, critique essays, and adapt explanations to each student. Early trials show better test scores and more help for students who are too embarrassed to ask questions in class.

But faculty worry students will outsource thinking. Some students feel watched and managed. Others say the old system already favored confident students with private tutors, so AI tutors make education fairer.

The university wants to know whether to adopt the system broadly, restrict it, or redesign courses around it. The decision involves learning quality, fairness, institutional trust, and the meaning of education.
~~~
