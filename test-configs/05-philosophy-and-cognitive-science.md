# 05-philosophy-and-cognitive-science

## Topic

Is the self a real thing, or just a useful story the brain tells?
## Context

~~~text
I am interested in this both philosophically and practically. Some traditions say the self is an illusion. Cognitive science often describes the self as a constructed model. But day to day, I still experience myself as a continuous person with memories, preferences, responsibilities, and relationships.

I want the discussion to distinguish between several possible meanings of "self": subjective experience, narrative identity, executive control, moral responsibility, and social identity. I also care about what changes if the self is partly constructed. Does that make personal growth easier, moral responsibility weaker, or suffering less sticky?

Please avoid a purely mystical answer or a purely reductionist answer unless a mind can defend why that frame is enough.
~~~
