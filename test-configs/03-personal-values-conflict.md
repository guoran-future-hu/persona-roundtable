# 03-personal-values-conflict

## Topic

Should I move abroad for career compounding when my family may need me soon?
## Context

~~~text
I am 29 and recently got an offer to move from my home country to Singapore for a high-growth role at a well-funded company. The role would put me around much stronger peers, give me regional exposure, and probably compress five years of learning into two. It is the kind of opportunity I wanted when I was younger.

At the same time, my parents are getting older. My father had a health scare last year. He is stable now, but it made the future feel less abstract. My mother says she supports me leaving, but I can tell she is scared. My younger sibling is also going through a difficult period and often relies on me to mediate family problems.

The job would pay about 70% more than my current role and could change my long-term network. Staying would mean a slower career path, but I would be physically present for my family and could help stabilize things. Remote work is possible in theory, but the Singapore role expects in-person intensity for at least the first year.

I feel pulled by two fears:

1. If I stay, I may resent my family and later blame them for a smaller life.
2. If I leave, I may become the kind of person who optimizes career while outsourcing love and duty to occasional visits.
3. If I try to split the difference, I may do both badly.

I am not asking for permission to choose one side. I want to understand what I might regret under each choice, what assumptions I should test before deciding, and whether there is a third path that is not just a weak compromise.
~~~
