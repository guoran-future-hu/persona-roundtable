# 01-technology-judgment

## Topic

Do current AI systems have anything meaningfully close to consciousness?
## Context

~~~text
I am not asking for a simple yes or no. I want to understand which definitions of consciousness matter and whether current AI systems satisfy any of them.

Assume we are discussing modern language models that can reason, remember within a context window, use tools, reflect on their outputs, and interact over long sessions. They do not have biological bodies, persistent personal experience by default, or clear evidence of subjective feeling.

I care about both the philosophical question and the practical consequences. If AI is not conscious, what mistakes do people make by treating it as if it is? If AI might become conscious, what signs would actually matter?
~~~
